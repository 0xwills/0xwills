## What is 0xWills?

0xWills helps crypto holders plan what happens to their assets when they die — without sharing seed phrases or giving up custody.

- **Non-custodial** — assets stay in your Gnosis Safe while you are alive
- **Email beneficiaries** — heirs do not need wallets upfront
- **Dead man's switch** — periodic check-ins prove you are alive
- **Trusted verifiers** — human confirmation before any transfer
- **Multi-chain** — ETH, ERC-20, Base, Arbitrum, and Bitcoin (timelocks)
